Quality & Export Service

Quality Control

Agreed in Writing, Checked Before Shipping

As a trading company our reputation depends on the mill's output — so we manage specification agreement and inspection actively, not by trust alone.

📝

1. Specification Agreement

Weight, width, material, finishing, packing and tolerance are confirmed in writing before production starts. No verbal specs.

🔍

2. In-Process Checks

For larger orders we follow up with the mill during production so deviations are caught early, not at packing time.

📷

3. Pre-Shipment Inspection

Every batch is checked against the agreed specification. Photos and available test data are shared with you before the goods leave.

What We Check

Inspection items depend on the product family — typical examples:

  • Area weight (GSM) and thickness against specification
  • Roll width and length / bag dimensions
  • Visual quality: defects, contamination, finish uniformity
  • Air permeability class for air slide fabric
  • Seam, collar and cage workmanship on dust filter bags
  • Packing: inner packaging, labels, carton marks

Third-party inspection can be arranged at the buyer's request where applicable.

Export Support

  • Commercial invoice & packing list
  • Certificate of origin, Form E where applicable
  • Bill of lading / airway bill coordination
  • Product photos & available test reports before shipment
  • Container loading supervision for full-container orders

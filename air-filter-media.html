Overview

What This Product Is

Synthetic air filter media is the raw material behind most general ventilation filtration: it is folded into bag and pocket filters, framed into box and panel filters, and cut into pads for air cleaners and paint booths. The fibre structure is built in graduated density — coarser fibres face the airflow, finer fibres lie deeper — so dust is held through the depth of the media instead of caking on the surface.

We supply the full EN 779 / ISO 16890 range. If your tender still specifies an F-grade alone, quote it as the combined F + ISO class (for example F7 / ePM1 55%) so every offer you receive is directly comparable. Roll widths run to 2.1 m and every roll ships with a data sheet covering grade, area weight and initial pressure drop.

FAQ

Common Questions

Which grade should I choose?
Match the grade to the room, not the catalogue: F5–F6 for pre-filtration and machine intake, F7 as the workhorse for occupied spaces, F8–F9 where the process demands it (hospitals, electronics, food areas).
Can I get media cut to my frame size?
Yes — send the frame dimensions and we cut pads or sheets to size; for volume orders we confirm the most economical cutting layout first.
Do you have media for rail and transit projects?
Yes. Flame-retardant synthetic media selected for DIN 5510-2 fire performance is available, produced by mills experienced in rail orders.

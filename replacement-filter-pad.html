Overview

What This Product Is

Most filtration maintenance is pad replacement: the frame stays, the media goes. We supply pads cut to your exact frame size in a range of thicknesses and efficiencies — from coarse washable lint pads for dryers and machine intakes to fine pads used as the final stage in small air handling units.

For maintenance buyers the economics are simple: order in the sizes you actually use, in packs that suit your stores, and standardise on one or two thicknesses across the plant. Send us your frame dimensions and we will propose the most practical size set and confirm the cutting layout before production.

FAQ

Common Questions

Can I order just one odd size?
Yes — pads are cut from roll media, so unusual sizes carry little penalty. Send the dimensions and we will confirm price and pack size.
How many times can a washable pad be reused?
It depends on the dust load; as a practical matter, rinse when the pressure drop rises and replace when the media no longer springs back. Most users get multiple cycles from a washable primary pad.
What is the MOQ for custom-cut pads?
Low — cut pads are a conversion of roll stock. The MOQ confirmed with your quotation depends on size and pack count.

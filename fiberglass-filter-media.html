Overview

What This Product Is

Fiberglass media covers two jobs synthetics struggle with. First, paint mist separation: the open, resilient glass fiber structure arrests wet overspray droplets while letting air pass at low resistance — the standard medium for spray booth exhaust and floor filtration. Second, high-temperature air: glass fiber keeps its structure where polymer media would soften or melt, making it the default for oven exhaust and hot process air.

Both are supplied as rolls for workshops that cut their own pads, or as finished pads sized to your holding frames. If you are converting from synthetic paint-stop media, tell us your current thickness and change-out interval — we will match the feel and improve on the holding capacity.

FAQ

Common Questions

Paint-stop fiberglass or synthetic — which should I use?
Fiberglass remains the reference for wet overspray because it does not absorb solvent and keeps its loft. Synthetic paint media can be cheaper per pad but loads differently; send your booth type and we will recommend based on duty.
What is the maximum continuous temperature?
It depends on the specific grade — our high-temperature grades are selected for oven-duty air. Confirm the exact limit on the data sheet supplied with your quotation.
Can you supply pads already cut?
Yes. Send the holding-frame dimensions; for volume orders we confirm the cutting layout to minimise waste.

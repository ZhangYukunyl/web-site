Overview

What This Product Is

The filter bag is the working core of every dust collector. Ours are built from needle-punched felt with a woven scrim support, and the surface finish is chosen for your duty: singed faces shed the dust cake cleanly, PTFE membranes capture sub-micron emissions, and repellent finishes keep moist or sticky dust from blinding the fabric.

Fitment is where most bag orders go wrong, so we treat it as a specification item, not an assumption. Tell us the collector model or send the bag dimensions — diameter, length, and whether the tubesheet takes a snap band, flange or raw-top collar — and we confirm a drawing with you before production. Every batch is inspected against that drawing before shipment.

FAQ

Common Questions

How do I order to size?
Send the bag's diameter × length and fitment type, or simply a photo of the collector nameplate. We confirm a dimension drawing with you before production.
Can you match the bag I currently use?
Yes — send a sample or the existing specification. We match construction, finish and fitment, and will point out any upgrade worth considering (for example a membrane finish where emissions limits are tight).
What is the minimum order quantity?
MOQ depends on the fabric and size; trial quantities are possible for standard constructions. The exact MOQ is confirmed with your quotation.
